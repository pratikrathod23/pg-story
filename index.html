A Letter From My Heart 💌

Dear Gauri ❤️,


I don't know whether you still remember our school days. Maybe they were just small memories for you, but for me... they became one of the most beautiful chapters of my life.


Back then we were just kids. We didn't understand what love really meant. Still... my heart somehow knew that you were special.


Time changed. Life changed. We got separated. But memories never left.


Today I don't expect anything from you. I only wanted to tell you... Thank you. Thank you for every smile, every conversation, every memory.


No matter where life takes us, I truly wish you happiness. Because seeing you happy will always make me smile.


Forever Grateful ❤️
